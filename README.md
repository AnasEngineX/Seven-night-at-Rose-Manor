## English

**Seven Nights at Rose Manor** is a psychological gothic mystery visual novel built on the **Ren'Py 8.5+** engine (Python 3).

---

### Version Information
- **Current Version**: `v1.0.0`
- **Release Status**: Full Story & Investigation System Release (7 Complete Nights, Branching Endings, Evidence Showcase & Victorian Soundscape)
- **Engine**: Ren'Py 8.5.2+ (Python 3.10+)
- **Supported Languages**: English & Bahasa Indonesia

---

### Synopsis
A cryptic invitation draws an investigator to Rose Manor—a secluded Victorian estate veiled in dark aristocratic secrets and long-buried occult rituals.

A violent tempest severs all access to the valley for seven consecutive nights. Each passing night unleashes mounting dread, ghostly whispers, and mysterious fatalities among the manor's occupants. Behind Victoria's icy composure, Evelyn's enigmatic smile, Arthur's guarded silence, and Father Thomas's grim prayers lies a harrowing truth that must be brought to light before the dawn of the seventh night.

---

### Key Features

#### 1. Evidence Journal & Visual Inspection System
- Collect clues, ancient tomes, occult relics, and forensic notes across seven nights.
- Features **Full Visual Inspection**, allowing players to examine high-resolution clue illustrations alongside investigative notes, occult tags, and discovery dates.

#### 2. Character Trust & Affection Dynamics
- Dialogue choices and investigative decisions influence the Trust and Affection levels of Rose Manor's residents:
  - **Evelyn Rosewood**: The eldest daughter, welcoming on the surface but guarding painful past secrets.
  - **Victoria Rosewood**: An authoritative, cold matriarch who views outsiders with deep suspicion.
  - **Arthur Pendelton**: The manor's devoted caretaker, loyal keeper of its darkest architectural secrets.
  - **Father Thomas**: A reclusive clergyman burdened by Rose Manor's forbidden past and heretical rites.

#### 3. Branching Storylines & Multiple Endings
- Player deductions, gathered evidence, and interpersonal relationships dictate the fate of the investigator and Rose Manor's inhabitants:
  - *True Ending*: Expose the mastermind and dismantle the manor's curse.
  - *Tragic Endings*: Succumb to the eternal curse or fall victim to occult intrigue.
  - *Escape Ending*: Flee the manor alive, leaving its lingering horrors unresolved.

#### 4. Victorian Gothic Soundscape & Period UI
- Custom audio design tailored for period immersion: crisp brass tactile button feedback, delicate parchment page turns, antique leather journal bindings, document taps, and gothic musical chimes for narrative choices.
- Atmospheric original score that shifts dynamically between daylight investigations and nighttime horrors.

#### 5. Full Bilingual Support
- Instant, seamless in-game language switching between **English** and **Bahasa Indonesia** via the Preferences menu.

### Running from Source Code

#### Prerequisites
1. Download and install **Ren'Py 8.5.0 or newer** from the official [Ren'Py Website](https://www.renpy.org/).
2. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.

#### Launching via Ren'Py Launcher
1. Open the **Ren'Py Launcher**.
2. Select **Preferences** in the lower-right corner and ensure the project directory path includes this project's folder.
3. Select **Seven Nights at Rose Manor** from the project list on the left.
4. Click **Launch Project** to begin playing.

#### Script Lint Verification
To verify the integrity of the project scripts via command line:
```bash
renpy.exe "path/to/Seven Nights at Rose Manor" lint
```

### Sinopsis Cerita
Sebuah undangan misterius membawa seorang penyelidik ke Rose Manor—sebuah kastil megah terpencil bergaya Victorian yang diselimuti rahasia kelam keluarga bangsawan serta misteri okultisme masa lalu.

Badai dahsyat yang melanda memutus seluruh akses keluar dari lembah Rose Manor selama tujuh malam berturut-turut. Setiap malam membawa ketegangan baru, bisikan-bisikan tak kasat mata, dan kematian misterius yang mengguncang para penghuni manor. Di balik tatapan dingin Victoria, senyum misterius Evelyn, kewaspadaan Arthur, dan doa-doa gelap Pastor Thomas, tersimpan kebenaran kelam yang menuntut untuk diungkapkan sebelum malam ketujuh berakhir.

---

### Fitur Utama Permainan

#### 1. Sistem Jurnal Investigasi & Berkas Barang Bukti (Evidence Journal)
- Mengumpulkan barang bukti, dokumen kuno, artefak okultisme, dan catatan forensik sepanjang tujuh malam.
- Dilengkapi dengan fitur **Inspeksi Visual Penuh** yang memungkinkan pemain meneliti ilustrasi barang bukti dalam resolusi tinggi beserta catatan analisis detail dan tanggal penemuan.

#### 2. Sistem Hubungan & Kepercayaan Karakter (Trust & Affection System)
- Setiap pilihan dialog dan tindakan investigasi memengaruhi tingkat kepercayaan (*Trust*) serta kedekatan (*Affection*) masing-masing penghuni manor:
  - **Evelyn Rosewood**: Putri tertua keluarga yang tampak ramah namun menyimpan rahasia masa lalu.
  - **Victoria Rosewood**: Sosok bangsawan dingin dan penuh otoritas yang mencurigai setiap langkah penyelidik.
  - **Arthur Pendelton**: Pengurus setia manor yang menjaga rahasia terdalam kediaman.
  - **Pastor Thomas**: Rohaniwan yang mengetahui sejarah kelam dan ritual terlarang Rose Manor.

#### 3. Percabangan Cerita & Ragam Ending (Branching Storylines)
- Keputusan pemain, barang bukti yang berhasil ditemukan, serta tingkat kepercayaan karakter menentukan nasib akhir penyelidik dan penghuni Rose Manor dalam berbagai konklusi cerita:
  - *True Ending*: Mengungkap dalang sebenarnya dan mematahkan kutukan Rose Manor.
  - *Tragic Endings*: Terjebak dalam kutukan abadi manor atau menjadi korban intrik okultisme.
  - *Escape Ending*: Melarikan diri dari manor namun meninggalkan misteri yang tak terpecahkan.

#### 4. Lanskap Suara & Desain Antarmuka Victorian (Atmospheric Soundscape & UI)
- Efek suara interaktif yang dibuat khusus untuk nuansa gotik klasik: klik mekanis kuningan, gesekan halus kertas perkamen, sampul jurnal kulit, ketukan berkas investigasi, dan denting pilihan narasi.
- Komposisi musik latar atmosferik yang mengiringi pergantian waktu dari siang yang mencekam hingga malam yang penuh misteri.

#### 5. Dukungan Dwi-Bahasa Penuh (Bilingual Support)
- Mendukung peralihan bahasa secara instan antara **Bahasa Indonesia** dan **English** melalui menu pengaturan (Preferences).
